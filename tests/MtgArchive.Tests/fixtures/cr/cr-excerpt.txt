1. Game Concepts

100. General

100.1. A test game involves two or more players.

100.1a A two-player test game begins with only two players.

100.1b A multiplayer test game begins with more than two players. See section 8, “Multiplayer Rules.”

100.2. Each player needs a deck, tokens to represent counters, and a way to track life totals.

Example: A player may track life totals on paper.

119.1. Each player begins the game with a starting life total.

704. State-Based Actions

704.5. The test state-based actions are as follows.

704.5a If a player has 0 or less life, that player loses the game.
