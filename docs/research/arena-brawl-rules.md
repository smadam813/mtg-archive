# Research: Arena Brawl format rules (Standard Brawl, Brawl, Competitive Brawl)

**Question:** What are the official rules of each MTG Arena Brawl queue? For each queue: deck size and card pool, commander eligibility, color identity and singleton rules, game setup, the banlist, Alchemy rebalances, and any power-level concept that could take the place of Commander brackets.

Researched 2026-10-03. Each claim cites its source. The source list is at the end. **(U)** marks a claim that no primary source confirms. The text names the secondary source that makes the claim.

## Answer

- Brawl and Competitive Brawl have the same deck rules: 100 cards, singleton, color identity, 25 starting life, two players. The commander is a legendary creature, planeswalker, Vehicle, or Spacecraft [S2].
- Competitive Brawl differs from Brawl in three ways. It has no free mulligan. It has its own banlist of 10 cards, mostly commanders. Its matchmaking uses Constructed rank and rating, not deck power [S2].
- Competitive Brawl does not share the Brawl banlist. Wizards calls them separate formats, "like comparing Historic to Timeless" [S2]. The Brawl list has 36 cards on 2026-10-03 [S4, S24, S6].
- Standard Brawl is the 60-card queue with the Standard card pool. Wizards publishes almost no Arena-specific rules for it. The closest primary rule is the tabletop Brawl option, CR 903.12 [S5].
- Since 2026-09-22, no format on Arena has rebalanced (`A-`) versions of tabletop cards. Each queue uses the original printing [S3, S6]. Digital-only Alchemy cards stay legal in Brawl and Competitive Brawl [S3].
- Wizards publishes no bracket system for Arena. Brawl and Standard Brawl pair players by a hidden, commander-weighted deck power value plus skill rating [S3, S28]. Competitive Brawl pairs by Constructed rank and rating only [S2].

## 1. Deck size, card pool, and pool rule

**Brawl.** A deck has 1 commander and 99 other cards, 100 in total [S1]. The commander can be "any legendary creature or Planeswalker from a set currently on Arena" [S1]. The pool is every card on Arena, so the format does not rotate. The Arena formats page says: "in Historic Brawl, you build a deck using any cards on MTG Arena, not just Standard-legal cards" [S30]. Arena-only collections such as Arena Anthology 3 and 4 are legal in Brawl [S35].

The Brawl format page has two old details. Its metadata describes "60 card, singleton decks that combines the Standard and Commander formats". Its commander line omits Vehicles and Spacecraft. The page body says 100 cards [S1].

**Competitive Brawl.** It uses "most of the same rules as Brawl" [S2]. Decks have 100 cards [S2]. Wizards started "with almost every card being legal to include in the 99" [S2]. Digital-only Alchemy cards are legal [S3].

**Standard Brawl.** No current Arena page states the deck size. CR 903.12d sets the tabletop Brawl option at exactly 60 cards, commander included [S5]. Draftsim states 60 for the Arena queue (U) [X1]. The pool is Standard. The 2019 Arena formats page describes Brawl, before the rename, as built "from the Standard card pool" [S30]. In 2020, Wizards wrote that some commanders "will no longer be legal in Standard Brawl" after rotation [S31]. CR 903.12b says Brawl decks are "usually" built from Standard cards [S5].

Scryfall shows two more facts about Standard Brawl (U) [X3]:

- Arcane Signet and Command Tower are legal, although they are not legal in Standard. mtg.wiki claims the same [X4].
- Cards banned in Standard are legal in Standard Brawl, for example Vivi Ornitier and Cori-Steel Cutter.

**Rename.** Historic Brawl became Brawl in late 2023. A search snippet attributes the rename to Arena patch notes 2023.33.00 (U) [X5]. That page returned 403, and the support API no longer lists notes before 2024. Primary sources set the bounds. The 2023-09-11 article still says "Historic Brawl & Brawl" [S28]. The 2024-11-04 article uses "Brawl" for the large format [S29]. "Ranked Brawl" became "Competitive Brawl" on 2026-06-08 [S2].

## 2. Commander eligibility

**Card types.** All three queues allow a legendary creature, a legendary planeswalker, a legendary Vehicle, or a legendary Spacecraft with power and toughness:

- Competitive Brawl, which states "Like Brawl" [S2].
- Arena patch notes from 2025-07-31: "All Legendary spacecraft and vehicles with defined power and toughness are eligible Commanders" [S8].
- The Arena announcement from 2025-06-23 says this starts with Edge of Eternities [S9].
- CR 903.12c lists the same four types for the Brawl option [S5].

**"Can be your commander."** CR 903.3a covers cards with this ability [S5]. No Arena source states how Arena handles them (U).

**Partner.** The 2025-06-30 B&R announcement says the FINAL FANTASY release "included the partner mechanic for the first time on MTG Arena" [S10]. Later patch notes extend it:

- 2026-02-23: the deck builder supports "Partner - Character Select" [S11].
- 2026-03-30: a fix stops a deck that pairs Partner—Character select with Partner [S13]. This matches CR 702.124f.
- 2026-09-09: a fix for "Partner With" commanders [S12].

The B&R text sits in its Brawl section, and it adds that matchmaking accounts for partner [S10]. The patch notes do not name a queue, so partner in Standard Brawl and Competitive Brawl is (U). CR 702.124c makes the color identity the union of both commanders [S5].

**Background.** No Arena source confirms "choose a Background" (U). In 2022, Arena made specialize "our take on the background mechanic". A specialize card can be the commander. The player picks a color, and that color sets the deck's color identity [S16].

**Companion.** "Each Historic Brawl deck may include a chosen companion. It starts outside the game and doesn't count as one of your 100 cards." The commander must also meet the companion's deck-building rule [S14]. Fixes from 2026-08-17 and 2026-08-18 confirm that companions work in Brawl and Competitive Brawl [S7, S15]. CR 702.139 gives the special action: pay {3} at sorcery speed to put the companion into your hand [S5]. CR 903.11a stops a card from outside the game if its color identity is outside the commander's [S5]. No source covers Standard Brawl (U).

## 3. Color identity, singleton, and colorless commanders

**Color identity.** "Cards must match the color identity of your commander" [S2]. CR 903.4 defines color identity. CR 903.5c applies it to deck building [S5]. The Brawl format page uses the looser words "same color as commander card" [S1].

**Singleton.** "Other than basic lands, only one copy of any given card can be in the deck (unless otherwise stated on the card)" [S2]. A 2025-07-16 fix made Brawl decks accept cards that say "A deck can use any number of…" [S18]. CR 903.5b states the same rule [S5].

**Colorless commander.** CR 903.12e: "If a player's commander has no colors in its color identity, that player's deck may contain any number of basic lands of one basic land type of their choice" [S5]. No Arena source states this rule for any queue (U). A 2025-07-08 fix made Wastes legal in Standard Brawl [S17].

## 4. Game setup

**Starting life.** Brawl: 25 [S1]. Competitive Brawl: 25 [S2]. Standard Brawl: CR 903.12f gives 25 in a two-player Brawl game [S5]. Draftsim also says 25 (U) [X1].

**Mulligan.** Brawl: "Best of one, including a free mulligan" [S1]. Competitive Brawl: "there is no free mulligan in Competitive Brawl, like other ranked formats" [S2]. This differs from CR 903.12g, which gives a free first mulligan in every Brawl game [S5]. Standard Brawl: CR 903.12g gives the free mulligan [S5]. Draftsim also says yes (U) [X1].

**Draw on turn 1.** CR 103.8a: "In a two-player game, the player who plays first skips the draw step... of their first turn" [S5]. CR 903 does not change this rule for Commander or Brawl. All three queues are two-player games, so the starting player does not draw on turn 1. No Arena source states this (U).

**Seats.** Two players in each queue. Sources: the Brawl format page ("Number of Players 2") [S1], the Competitive Brawl matchmaking text [S2], and the Arena formats page ("one-on-one matches") [S30].

**Commander damage.** CR 903.12h removes the 21-damage loss from the Brawl option [S5]. No Arena source covers the 100-card queues (U).

## 5. Banlists

**Where Wizards announces them.** The scheduled B&R announcements on magic.wizards.com have a "Brawl" section [S10, S19, S22, S23]. Since 2026-06-29, they also have a "Competitive Brawl" section [S22]. Pre-bans for new sets appear in Arena announcements and patch notes [S24, S6]. The format article can also carry bans [S3]. The standing list is at `magic.wizards.com/en/banned-restricted-list` [S4]. The next B&R announcement is on 2026-10-12 [S23].

The standing list was out of date on 2026-10-03. It does not show Eye of Ugin under Brawl. Wizards announced that ban on 2026-09-14, and it took effect on 2026-09-22 [S24, S6].

**Brawl list (36 cards on 2026-10-03).** Both lists say a banned card "cannot be included in your deck or used as your commander" [S4].

Agent of Treachery; Ancient Tomb; Chalice of the Void; Channel; Chrome Mox; Deadly Rollick; Deflecting Swat; Demonic Tutor; Drannith Magistrate; Eye of Ugin [S24, S6]; Field of the Dead; Fierce Guardianship; Flawless Maneuver; Force of Will; Gideon's Intervention; Iona, Shield of Emeria; Lutri, the Spellchaser; Mana Drain; Meddling Mage; Nadu, Winged Wisdom; Natural Order; Nexus of Fate; Obscuring Haze; Oko, Thief of Crowns; Phyrexian Revoker; Pithing Needle; Runed Halo; Sorcerous Spyglass; Strip Mine; Subtlety; Tainted Pact; Temporal Manipulation; Time Warp; Ugin, the Spirit Dragon; Ugin's Labyrinth; Wash Away [S4].

Scryfall also lists Disruptor Flute as banned in Brawl (U) [X3]. No Wizards source found names it.

**Brawl philosophy.** Brawl bans cards in the 99 that "homogenize the format, are too efficient, or are simply polarizing". It bans few commanders, because matchmaking pairs commanders of similar power [S2, S3]. Wizards banned Strip Mine, Mana Drain, Chrome Mox, and Ancient Tomb on 2025-11-10 [S19]. It banned six more cards on 2026-06-29 [S22]. It banned Nadu on 2026-09-22 [S3].

**Competitive Brawl list (10 cards).** Ajani, Nacatl Pariah; Lutri, the Spellchaser; Nadu, Winged Wisdom; Oko, Thief of Crowns; Old Stickfingers; Ragavan, Nimble Pilferer; Rusko, Clockmaker; Tajic, Legion's Valor; Tamiyo, Inquisitive Student; Wrenn and Six [S2, S4].

Wizards built this list by banning commanders "above a certain power". It expects "a light banned card list for cards in the 99" and a power level "closer to Timeless's" [S2, S3]. The list has had no change since launch [S22, S23, S3]. Wizards grants wildcard refunds for future bans in Brawl and Competitive Brawl [S2].

**Standard Brawl.** The standing list has no Standard Brawl section [S4]. The B&R announcements do not mention it [S10, S19, S22, S23]. Draftsim says "There are no cards banned in Standard Brawl" (U) [X1]. Scryfall shows no bans [X3].

**Banned as commander.** Since 2026-08-17, all three queues support a ban that applies only to the commander slot: "Cards 'banned as Commander' can still be played in one's maindeck for that format" [S7]. On 2026-10-03, no published list uses this status [S4].

## 6. Alchemy rebalances

**Before 2026-09-22.** For a printed card, Wizards made a separate digital card with an `A-` prefix, for example A-Esika's Chariot [S26]. The `A-` version was legal "in Historic, Historic Brawl, and similar digital-only formats". Formats played on tabletop, such as Standard, kept the printed card [S26]. Three sources show that Brawl used the `A-` version:

- A 2026-01-13 fix stopped "Friendly Brawl" from reverting rebalanced cards [S27].
- The 2026-05-18 B&R says A-Vivi Ornitier "is currently well positioned" in Brawl [S21].
- The December 2025 competitive test event banned "A-Nadu, Winged Wisdom" [S20]. So the `A-` card was also the version in the competitive pool.

No source names Standard Brawl. As a Standard-based format, it probably used the printed card (U).

**Since 2026-09-22.** "All rebalanced cards with tabletop printings will be reverted to their original printings across all formats... Those cards will match their tabletop versions in all formats where they are legal" [S3, S6]. So no queue has an `A-` version of a tabletop card now. Each queue uses the original card, subject to its banlist. Digital-only Alchemy cards stay legal in Historic, Timeless, Brawl, and Competitive Brawl. Wizards "may choose to ban or rebalance digital-only Alchemy cards in the future" [S3]. A digital-only card gets its rebalance as a text change in place, with no new name [S26].

**Alchemy end dates.** Alchemy: Reality Fracture, on 2026-10-13, is the last Alchemy set. The Alchemy format leaves Arena on 2027-02-02 [S3].

## 7. Power level and matchmaking in place of brackets

Wizards publishes no bracket list, tier list, or deck score for any Arena queue. The power concept is internal to matchmaking.

**Brawl and Standard Brawl.** In 2023, Wizards wrote: "we use Commander-based matchmaking in Brawl modes" [S28]. "Brawl modes" then covered Standard Brawl and Historic Brawl. The current Brawl system "considers the power of each player's deck, mostly weighted by commander choice, and each player's skill rating across Constructed formats" [S3]. Patch notes refer to per-commander values: "Brawl commander values" (2024-07-09) and "Commander point value" (2025-08-18) [S32].

Wizards changes the weights often:

- 2026-05-19: matchmaking used commander tiers and ignored skill [S21, S33].
- Wizards then added player rating back "to a lesser extent than before". It made four more changes after that [S2].
- Wizards does not publish the values. Draftsim calls them deck "weight" and says a 2024 bug exposed them (U) [X6].

No source describes a separate value for Standard Brawl (U).

**Competitive Brawl.** Matchmaking uses "a player's Constructed rank and rating". "Deck composition is not a factor for matchmaking" [S2]. Power control comes from the banlist alone.

**Queue choice as intent.** The queue itself signals intent. Players "opt into their ideal experience", and the queue "tells the opponent what type of match they are entering" [S3]. Wizards calls Brawl casual: it "puts fun first" [S29]. Competitive Brawl aims at a power level near Timeless [S3].

## Open and unverified points

1. **Standard Brawl rules.** No Arena-specific primary source gives the deck size, life, mulligan, banlist, or commander damage. The answers above come from CR 903.12 and secondary sites.
2. **Arcane Signet and Command Tower in Standard Brawl.** Only Scryfall and mtg.wiki state this [X3, X4].
3. **Disruptor Flute.** Scryfall lists it as banned in Brawl [X3]. Wizards' list does not.
4. **Colorless commanders.** No Arena source states the basic-land rule for any queue. CR 903.12e covers only the tabletop Brawl option.
5. **Commander damage, turn-1 draw, and best of one** for the 100-card queues. Only the CR or inference supports these. Arena sources say nothing.
6. **Background.** No Arena source confirms "choose a Background". Arena used specialize instead in 2022 [S16].
7. **Rename date.** The exact date of the Historic Brawl rename is not confirmed. The bounds are 2023-09-11 to 2024-11-04 [S28, S29].
8. **Out-of-date pages.** The Brawl format page and the standing banlist page lag behind the announcements [S1, S4]. A data pipeline must not use them as the only source.

## Facts for the follow-up tickets

These are facts, not decisions.

- CR 903.12 describes a 60-card, Standard-based Brawl. It matches Arena's Standard Brawl most closely. Wizards does not tie the 100-card queues to any CR section [S5].
- The XMage shim's `GoldfishDuel` forces a turn-1 draw (charting facts). CR 103.8a gives no turn-1 draw to the starting player in a two-player game [S5].
- Competitive Brawl has no free mulligan [S2]. Brawl and the CR Brawl option have one [S1, S5].
- On 2026-10-03, Scryfall exposed `standardbrawl`, `brawl`, and `competitivebrawl` legality keys. It already showed the Eye of Ugin ban. Exact-name lookups for A-Nadu, A-Vivi Ornitier, and A-Esika's Chariot returned 404 [X3]. This data question stays open.
- The map's charting facts say the Brawl pool includes "Alchemy and rebalanced cards". The rebalanced part is out of date since 2026-09-22 [S3, S6].
